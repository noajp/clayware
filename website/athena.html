Athena is your team’s work assistant. Anyone on the team can ask it for work.

Write what you need the way you’d ask a teammate. Athena plans, searches the web, records in tables and drafts documents. Judgment and approval are done by people. What was asked, decided and done stays in the team’s record.

Searches the web
Gathers what’s needed from the web. Each claim carries its source and when it was read; what couldn’t be confirmed is listed separately.
Records in tables
Customers, candidates and inquiries are recorded as rows in a table.
Writes documents
Drafts reports and replies to mail.
Operates a browser
On a registered computer, it also opens a browser and fills in forms.
Splits it into work
Creates Elements for the work to be done from what it gathered.
Checks against the goal
Checks whether the work in progress fits the Strategy’s success criteria.

What AI does and what people decide

Research, drafting, totals and analysis are done by Athena. Judgment, approval and commitments to people outside are done by people.

WorkWhat Athena doesWhat a person decides
InquiriesClassifies each inquiry, records it in a table and drafts a replyWhether to send the reply
Morning summaryTotals the previous day and lists open and high-risk items firstWhich to handle first
Complaint analysisClassifies causes and proposes improvementsWhich proposal to adopt
Sales follow-upDrafts the message after a meetingWhat to send
DevelopmentChanges code in an isolated environment and opens a pull request after the tests passWhether to merge

Defining how the work is done (harness)

Athena follows a harness written for each kind of work.

What it holdsContents
When to use itA name, a one-line description, when to use it and when not to
InputsThe inputs needed and their form. It does not run until they are all there
StepsThe order of operations and the branches. Conditions checked by machine are written apart from the points left to AI’s judgment
RulesAllowed operations, how reads and writes are treated, what needs approval, limits on counts, allowed recipients, how owners are chosen
Done and failureWhat counts as done, what must not be done, and what to do on failure
A harness is checked when it is saved. Ready-made harnesses: inquiry intake and routing, the morning inquiry summary, complaint analysis and improvement proposals, sales follow-up.

Work continues while the app is closed

  1. Starts on its ownAt a set time, or on a notice from outside (webhook)
  2. With the starter’s permissionsReads and writes only what the person who started it can see
  3. Resumes where it stoppedIf it stops for an approval or a time limit, it continues from the last record
  4. Asks a person when information is missingPuts a question with choices in the Inbox and resumes when it is answered

How AI is used safely

Only what was approved runs
Approval is bound to the exact input that runs. AI cannot approve its own work.
A second AI checks
A result is checked by AI in a separate context before it counts as done. What couldn’t be checked is marked unverified.
It stops on its own
Bursts of writes to the same target, rising errors, or a first send to a new recipient stop the run and pass it to a person.
Spending limits
It stops at the Space’s monthly budget and at the per-run limit.
Choose what is handed over
The organization chooses whether each operation is confirmed or handed over (always ask, automatic, don’t ask).
Choose the model
Switch models for speed or accuracy, to suit the work.
