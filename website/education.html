Comprehensive support for using AI, transforming the work, and helping each person through the change.

Whether or not you use Clayware, we combine, to fit your organization, the knowledge to bring AI into the work, a review of how the work is done, and support for each employee as they move to the new way of working (change management).

Three areas of support

  1. Using AIKnowledge and practice

    What generative AI can and can’t do, how to ask for work and check the results, handling information and risk, and writing internal rules. Not limited to any one product.

  2. Transforming the workReviewing how it’s done

    Map the work, divide what AI does from what people judge, set success criteria in numbers, then try and fix the new steps, using your own work as the material.

    About change management

  3. Change management for each personMoving one person at a time

    Hear the worries and load the new way brings, confirm changes in each person’s role with them, and set up practice and someone to talk to until it sticks. Support is per person, not only per team.

Who it is for, and the format

WhoContentFormat

StaffPractice on your own work: asking AI, checking results, sending work backHands-on
Internal championsHow to design, test and run automation, and how to take the next process forward in-houseSeveral sessions
Each employeeInterviews, practice and consultation about moving to the new way of workingOne to one

How we work

The material is your actual work, not practice examples.

  1. Hear the current stateThe work, how AI is used today, and what worries or burdens staff
  2. Set goals and whoDecide who should learn what, by when
  3. DeliverCombine training, hands-on sessions and one-to-one interviews and practice
  4. ReviewCheck how the work and the people have changed, and plan the next step

What you will be able to do

Asking AI
How to request work from AI by giving the purpose and success criteria.
Checking results
How to check the reasons and sources behind AI’s results and judge whether they are right.
Sending work back
How to say concretely what is wrong and have it redone.
Setting approval
How to set the line between what people decide and what AI is handed.
Reviewing the work
How to map the work, set success criteria and keep fixing the steps.
Facing change
How the person and the organization handle the worry and load of moving to a new way.
